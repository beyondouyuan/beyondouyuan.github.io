Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. FamilyPet has no account system and no login. Open the app and start adding your pets right away.

Where is my data stored, and how do I back it up or move to a new phone?

All records (profiles, vaccine and deworming schedules, weight, medical notes, photos) are stored only in a local database on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

To back up or move devices, go to Settings → Data Export and export as CSV or JSON, then save the file to Files, iCloud Drive, or send it to yourself. On the new phone, import the backup file. Uninstalling the app deletes the local database, so export first.

My reminders are not arriving.
  1. Check iOS Settings → Notifications → FamilyPet and make sure notifications are allowed, with banners or alerts enabled.
  2. In the app, open the reminder you expect and confirm the due date and reminder time are correct.
  3. If the phone was off or in Focus / Do Not Disturb at the reminder time, iOS may have held the notification; open the app once to refresh the schedule.

FamilyPet uses local notifications only. There is no server push, so reminders work fully offline.

Camera access was denied — how do I take a pet photo again?

Open iOS Settings → FamilyPet → Camera and switch it on. Then return to the pet profile and choose Take Photo again. Choosing a photo from the library does not require the camera permission.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records inside the app, or remove the app from your iPhone — that clears the local database and any avatar copies saved in the app's storage. Exported CSV/JSON files are under your control and must be deleted by you.

Because we never receive your data, there is no server-side copy to delete.

常见问题

需要注册账号吗？

不需要。全家宠没有账号体系，也无需登录，打开应用即可开始录入宠物档案。

数据存在哪里？如何备份或换手机？

所有数据（档案、疫苗与驱虫排期、体重、病历、头像）只保存在本机数据库中，不上传任何服务器。

备份或换机请走 设置 → 数据导出，导出 CSV 或 JSON 后保存到「文件」、iCloud 云盘，或发送给自己；在新手机上导入该备份文件即可。卸载应用会清除本地数据库，请先导出。

收不到提醒通知怎么办？
  1. 检查 iOS 设置 → 通知 → 全家宠，确认允许通知，且横幅/提醒样式已开启。
  2. 在应用内打开对应排期，确认到期日期与提醒时间是正确值。
  3. 若提醒时间点手机处于关机或专注/勿扰模式，系统可能未弹出；打开一次应用可刷新排期。

全家宠只使用本地通知，不依赖服务器推送，离线也能正常工作。

相机权限被拒绝了，怎么重新拍宠物照片？

打开 iOS 设置 → 全家宠 → 相机 并开启，然后回到宠物档案重新选择拍照。从相册选图不需要相机权限。

如何删除我的数据？

可在应用内逐条删除记录，或直接卸载应用——这会清除本地数据库和应用内的头像副本。已导出的 CSV/JSON 文件由你自行保管与删除。

因为我们从不接收你的数据，服务端没有可删除的副本。
