Frequently Asked Questions

How does the action verification work?

Thunder Alarm opens your camera when the reminder fires, runs a on-device pose-detection model (Google ML Kit), and confirms you completed the required action — head shake, nod, hand raise, squat or jumping jack — before the alarm stops. All processing happens locally; nothing is uploaded to a server.

Free vs. Pro — what's the difference?

Free: Head shake and nod actions, 3 recent history items, basic completion stats.

Pro: All 5 actions including hand raise, squat and jumping jack; full history with 7/30-day trend charts; customizable target counts. $4.99/month or $19.99/year.

My alarm did not go off — what do I check?
  1. Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Thunder Alarm — make sure notifications are allowed, with banners or alerts enabled, and Sounds is on.
  2. In the app, open the reminder you expect and confirm time, date and repeat schedule are correct.
  3. If your phone was in Focus / Do Not Disturb at the alarm time, iOS may have suppressed it. Add Thunder Alarm to your Focus exceptions or switch to an Alarm-type reminder (iOS can ring even in Focus).
  4. Open the app once — this refreshes the local notification schedule, which iOS may throttle if the app has not run for days.

Thunder Alarm uses local notifications only. No server push, so it works fully offline.

Camera access was denied — how do I enable it again?

Open iOS Settings → Thunder Alarm → Camera and switch it on. The app needs camera access only at the moment the reminder fires for a single pose-detection pass — no recording, no video stored.

Can I skip the action verification?

No — the whole point is to make snoozing hard. You can, however, choose an Alarm-type reminder (not Water or Medicine), select a simpler action (head shake or nod), or lower the target count down to 1 (Pro only).

Where is my data stored? Will it be deleted?

Reminders and history live in a local database on your device. Uninstalling the app clears both. There is no server copy to delete or export. iOS iCloud backups may include the app container unless you exclude it.

常见问题

动作验证是怎么回事？

闹钟响起时会打开相机，在本机运行 Google ML Kit 的姿态识别模型，确认你已完成指定动作（摆头 / 点头 / 举手 / 深蹲 / 开合跳）后才会停止闹铃。所有处理都在本地进行，不上传任何图像或数据到服务器。

免费版和 Pro 有什么区别？

免费版：摆头、点头两种动作，最近 3 条打卡历史，基础完成统计。

Pro：解锁举手、深蹲、开合跳；完整历史和 7/30 天趋势图；自定义目标次数。月 $4.99 / 年 $19.99。

闹钟没响怎么办？
  1. 检查 iOS 设置 → 通知 → 雷霆闹钟，确认允许通知，横幅/提醒样式已开启，且声音已打开。
  2. 在应用内打开对应提醒，确认时间、日期、重复规则都设对了。
  3. 若闹铃时间点手机处于专注/勿扰模式，系统可能压制通知——可把雷霆闹钟加入 Focus 例外，或改用「闹钟」类型（iOS 即使在专注模式也会响铃）。
  4. 打开一次应用——这会刷新本地通知调度（iOS 对长期未运行的 App 会节流）。

雷霆闹钟只使用本地通知，不依赖服务器推送，离线也能正常工作。

相机权限被拒绝了，怎么重新开？

打开 iOS 设置 → 雷霆闹钟 → 相机 并开启。相机只在提醒响起那一刻打开做一次姿态识别——不录像、不存图。

能跳过动作验证直接关掉闹钟吗？

不能——雷霆闹钟的核心就是让你赖不了床。但你可以换一种方式：把提醒类型改成「闹钟」而非喝水/吃药；选更简单的动作（摆头或点头）；在 Pro 版把目标次数降到 1。

数据存在哪里？会被删除吗？

提醒和打卡历史只保存在本机数据库，卸载应用会一并清除，服务器没有副本。iOS 设置里的 iCloud 备份可能包含应用容器——如果不想被备份进去，在 iCloud 管理页面手动排除。
