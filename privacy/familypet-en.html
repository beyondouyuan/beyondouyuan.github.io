Privacy Policy

FamilyPet · Pet Health Management

中文
Effective date: October 24, 2026  ·  Applies to: FamilyPet
We know how much your family's and your pets' health data matters to you. This policy explains how FamilyPet handles your data, with one core commitment throughout: your data stays on your device and never leaves it.

1. Our Core Commitment

Your data stays on your device: your pet health data — profiles, schedules, weight, medical records, and family members — is stored only on your device by default. No account is required, and nothing is sent to any server.

FamilyPet is built around a local-first principle. Pet health records are sensitive personal information, so we keep them on your device, entirely under your control.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Health data you create (stored only on this device)

Data typeStoragePurpose
Pet profiles (name, breed, birthday, photo)Local databaseBuild pet health profiles
Health schedules (vaccines, deworming) and remindersLocal databaseDue reminders and records
Weight recordsLocal databaseTrack growth trends
Medical recordsLocal databaseKeep a history of vet visits
Family member informationLocal databaseMultiple roles on the same phone

This data is stored in a local database on your device (drift SQLite) and is never uploaded to the cloud.

2.2 Usage statistics and behavioral tracking

FamilyPet does not collect usage statistics, behavioral tracking, device identifiers, or crash diagnostics. The app contains no analytics SDK and does not send data to any server operated by the developer.

2.3 Subscription and purchase information

Not applicable. The current version of FamilyPet is free in full and offers no in-app purchases, so no payment information is collected or processed at all.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide core features on your device, including pet health profiles, due reminders, weight and medical records, and multi-role collaboration on the same phone;
  • To ask your device for permission to send reminder notifications when you take that action.

4. Data Export and Your Control

Export means control: at any time you can export your local data as CSV / JSON files from Settings → Data Export. The files are created only on your device, and whether and how you share them (for example, through the system share sheet or the Files app) is entirely up to you.

Exported CSV files open in common spreadsheet apps. The JSON file contains all health data and can be used for backup or to move to another device (migration availability depends on the product version).

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your pet health data with any third party. We may disclose it only in the following cases:

  • Where required by laws and regulations, by judicial or administrative authorities;
  • Where necessary to protect our lawful rights or public safety.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • Local data: kept on your device until you delete it. You can delete records in the app or uninstall the app, which clears all local data.
  • Exported files: created by you and kept on your device or in a location you choose. FamilyPet cannot delete them remotely.

7. Children's Privacy

FamilyPet is intended for family use and may involve minors helping to care for a pet. Because the app does not collect personally identifying information and does not provide an account system, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your family's data has been mishandled, please contact us using the details below.

8. Data Security

  • The local database is stored inside the app sandbox and protected by the device's system security mechanisms;
  • You grant or revoke reminder notification permission yourself in system settings;
  • We follow a data-minimization principle: any data that can stay on your device is never uploaded.

9. Fees and In-App Purchases

  • FamilyPet is free in full in the current version: no in-app purchases and no ads.
  • If paid features are introduced later, their price and scope will be stated in the app and in this policy before the update, and will apply only after you confirm.
  • No existing feature is restricted by lack of payment.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced through an app update or on this page. The effective date of the policy appears at the top of this page.
