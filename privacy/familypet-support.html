Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. FamilyPet Health has no account system and no login. Open the app and start adding your pets right away.

Where is my data stored, and how do I back it up or move to a new phone?

All records (profiles, vaccine and deworming schedules, weight, medical notes, photos) are stored in a local database on your device by default. Nothing is uploaded to any server unless you explicitly start an iCloud backup.

The simplest backup is Settings → iCloud Backup: it writes a snapshot to your own private iCloud database (only you can read it; household members can't see it), and you can restore it on another device signed into the same Apple ID. Backup and restore are manual only — no automatic sync.

You can also export files via Settings → Data Export (CSV or JSON), then save them to Files, iCloud Drive, or send them to yourself. Uninstalling the app deletes the local database, so back up first.

I'm moving to a new iPhone — what is the correct order?

Two separate things travel with you, and they depend on two different Apple IDs:

  1. Subscription (App Store account). Sign in on the new phone with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. Premium unlocks automatically a few seconds after launch, once the app has checked with the App Store. If it does not, open Settings → Subscription and tap Restore Purchases.
  2. Your records (iCloud account). On the old phone open Settings → iCloud Backup and tap Back Up. On the new phone, sign in to the same iCloud account, open the same page, tap Restore, and confirm the overwrite.

Backup and restore are free for everyone, so you can move your records without an active subscription. Restore replaces the whole database instead of merging, so avoid editing the same data on two phones at the same time. Avatars are included in the snapshot; medical record photos are not.

Is FamilyPet Health free? What does the subscription include?

Core features are free forever and the app has no ads. The free version includes one pet profile with vaccine and deworming reminders, weight and medical records, local storage, CSV/JSON export, iCloud backup and restore, and basic vet cards.

FamilyPet Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription that unlocks multiple pet profiles, family sharing, and health trend reports. It is available monthly (US$1.99) or yearly (US$12.99); prices are shown in your local currency on the purchase screen before you confirm.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Apple ID you used to subscribe, then open Settings → Subscription in the app and tap Restore Purchases. If your subscription is still active, the premium features unlock immediately. If nothing is found, check that the subscription is active under iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

How do I cancel the subscription, and how do refunds work?

Cancel any time in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → FamilyPet Health, or from the manage-subscription link on the App Store. Canceling stops future renewals; premium features stay available until the end of the current paid period, then the app falls back to the free single-pet version. Your existing data is kept.

Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds directly because we never receive your payment details.

My reminders are not arriving.
  1. Check iOS Settings → Notifications → FamilyPet and make sure notifications are allowed, with banners or alerts enabled.
  2. In the app, open the reminder you expect and confirm the due date and reminder time are correct.
  3. If the phone was off or in Focus / Do Not Disturb at the reminder time, iOS may have held the notification; open the app once to refresh the schedule.

FamilyPet Health uses local notifications only. There is no server push, so reminders work fully offline.

Camera access was denied — how do I take a pet photo again?

Open iOS Settings → FamilyPet → Camera and switch it on. Then return to the pet profile and choose Take Photo again. Choosing a photo from the library does not require the camera permission.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records inside the app, or remove the app from your iPhone — that clears the local database and any avatar copies saved in the app's storage. Exported CSV/JSON files are under your control and must be deleted by you.

Because we never receive your data, there is no server-side copy to delete.

常见问题

需要注册账号吗？

不需要。全家宠没有账号体系，也无需登录，打开应用即可开始录入宠物档案。

数据存在哪里？如何备份或换手机？

所有数据（档案、疫苗与驱虫排期、体重、病历、头像）默认只保存在本机数据库中，除非你主动点击 iCloud 备份，否则不会上传任何服务器。

推荐备份方式：设置 → iCloud 备份，快照写入你本人的 iCloud 私有库（仅你自己可读，家人成员看不到），换设备时需登录同一个 Apple ID 才能恢复；备份与恢复均为手动操作，暂不自动同步。

也可以走 设置 → 数据导出，导出 CSV 或 JSON 后保存到「文件」、iCloud 云盘，或发送给自己。卸载应用会清除本地数据库，请先备份。

恢复前应用会自动把本机现状存成一份恢复点（最多保留 5 份），确认弹窗会提示恢复后将删除的宠物与记录；若恢复后发现不对，可在提示条点撤销，或到「数据导出」页的「恢复点（自动）」里回退。

换新 iPhone 时，正确的操作顺序是什么？

需要迁移的是两件事，分属两个不同的 Apple ID：

  1. 订阅权益（App Store 账号）：新手机登录订阅时使用的同一个 Apple ID。启动后应用会向 App Store 校验，几秒内自动解锁高级会员；若没有，打开 设置 → 订阅 点恢复购买。
  2. 你的数据（iCloud 账号）：先在旧手机打开 设置 → iCloud 备份 点备份；新手机登录同一个 iCloud 账号，在同一页面点恢复并确认覆盖。

备份与恢复对所有人免费，无需等订阅生效即可迁移数据。恢复是整库覆盖而非合并，不要在两台手机上同时编辑同一份数据。快照会包含头像，但不包含病历照片。

全家宠免费吗？订阅包含什么？

基础功能永久免费，且无广告。免费版可建立 1 只宠物档案，使用疫苗与驱虫提醒、体重与病历记录、本地存储，并可使用 CSV/JSON 导出、iCloud 备份与恢复与基础就诊卡。

FamilyPet Premium（全家宠高级会员）是可选自动续费订阅，解锁多宠物档案、家人共享与健康趋势报告。支持按月（US$1.99）或按年（US$12.99）订阅，实际价格以你确认前购买页显示的地区货币为准。

换手机后如何恢复已购买的订阅？

用订阅时的同一个 Apple ID 登录，打开应用 设置 → 订阅，点击恢复购买。订阅仍有效时会立即解锁权益。若提示未找到，请到 iOS 设置 → [你的姓名] → 订阅 确认订阅状态。

如何取消订阅？退款怎么处理？

可随时在 iOS 设置 → [你的姓名] → 订阅 → 全家宠 取消，或使用 App Store 的「管理订阅」入口。取消后不再续费，权益保留到当前付费周期结束，之后自动回到免费单宠版本，已有数据不会删除。

退款由 Apple 按其政策处理，请到 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请。由于我们从不接触你的支付信息，无法直接为你退款。

收不到提醒通知怎么办？
  1. 检查 iOS 设置 → 通知 → 全家宠，确认允许通知，且横幅/提醒样式已开启。
  2. 在应用内打开对应排期，确认到期日期与提醒时间是正确值。
  3. 若提醒时间点手机处于关机或专注/勿扰模式，系统可能未弹出；打开一次应用可刷新排期。

全家宠只使用本地通知，不依赖服务器推送，离线也能正常工作。

相机权限被拒绝了，怎么重新拍宠物照片？

打开 iOS 设置 → 全家宠 → 相机 并开启，然后回到宠物档案重新选择拍照。从相册选图不需要相机权限。

如何删除我的数据？

可在应用内逐条删除记录，或直接卸载应用——这会清除本地数据库和应用内的头像副本。已导出的 CSV/JSON 文件由你自行保管与删除。

因为我们从不接收你的数据，服务端没有可删除的副本。
