Privacy Policy

Thunder Alarm · Reminders + Action Verification

中文
Effective date: November 21, 2026  ·  Updated: November 21, 2026  ·  Applies to: Thunder Alarm
We know your time and focus matter. Thunder Alarm is a personal productivity tool that reminds you at the scheduled time and verifies the action through your camera. Our design principle is data never leaves your device: all reminders, action records, and preferences are stored locally only.

1. Our Core Commitment

Data stays on your device: your reminder settings, action completion records, and preferences are stored only on your device. No account is required and nothing is sent to any server.

Thunder Alarm is built around a local-first principle. The camera-based action verification (head shake / nod / hand raise, etc.) that triggers at reminder time is processed frame-by-frame on the device. Once the recognition result confirms you have completed the target number of actions, the raw frames are released immediately — we do not capture, store, or upload your camera footage or pose data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Reminder data you create (stored only on this device)

This data is stored locally on your device (drift SQLite + shared_preferences). It is never uploaded to any server and is not written to iCloud or other cloud backups. If you use a system-level device backup (iOS / Android), the backup is controlled by Apple or Google and Thunder Alarm has no access to it.

2.2 Camera frames and raw pose data

Frame-by-frame processing, discarded immediately: the camera turns on only when you enter verification. Frames are analyzed on the device by the pose recognition SDK. Once the target action count is confirmed, the raw frames are released immediately — never written to disk, never uploaded.

2.3 Local aggregated analytics (counts only)

Thunder Alarm records anonymous aggregate count events (such as "app launched", "reminder created", "validation completed") in the local database to understand feature usage. These events:

  • Stay in the local database and are never reported to any server;
  • Do not include camera footage, reminder body text, or any personally identifiable information;
  • Are cleared automatically when you uninstall the app.

2.4 Subscriptions, purchases, and account information

The current version is fully free: no in-app purchases and no account system. We do not collect any payment information, email address, or phone number.

4. Third-Party SDKs and Network Transmission

4.1 Action recognition SDKs

  • Android: Google ML Kit Pose Detection (google_mlkit_pose_detection)
  • iOS: Apple Vision framework (bridged through the Google ML Kit iOS package)

Pose recognition runs entirely on the device and does not make any network requests.

4.2 Transitive network libraries

Honest disclosure: the dependency tree of Google ML Kit Pose Detection includes three libraries — Google DataTransport, GTMSessionFetcher, and nanopb. These are standard Firebase / Google Analytics network transport components that are theoretically capable of making requests to Google servers.

Thunder Alarm does not initialize Firebase and does not call any reporting API. In our code paths, these libraries are only brought in as compile-time dependencies by the ML Kit package and do not make network requests by default. If Google silently reports telemetry inside their Pose Detection SDK (not under our control), please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

4.3 Remaining dependencies

  • Local storage: drift SQLite (encrypted in the app sandbox), shared_preferences (key-value storage)
  • Alarm scheduling: flutter_alarmkit (iOS AlarmKit native channel), flutter_local_notifications (local notification fallback)
  • Permission queries: permission_handler (queries only, no reporting)
  • Timezone: flutter_timezone (reads system timezone, no reporting)

None of the above dependencies contain code that actively initiates network requests. Thunder Alarm itself contains zero HTTP / HTTPS request logic, apart from any implicit behavior inside system or third-party SDKs that is outside our control.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. We may disclose it only in the following cases:

  • Where required by laws and regulations, by judicial or administrative authorities;
  • Where necessary to protect our lawful rights or public safety.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • Local data: kept on your device until you delete it. You can delete reminders in the app or uninstall the app (clearing all local data).
  • Raw camera frames: released after per-frame processing — never persisted.
  • Local aggregated analytics: shares the same lifecycle as local data and is cleared on uninstall.

7. Children's Privacy

Thunder Alarm is intended for adult users. Because the app does not collect personally identifying information and does not provide an account system, and because all data stays on the device, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe any data has been mishandled, please contact us using the details below.

8. Data Security

  • The local database is stored inside the app sandbox and protected by the device's system security mechanisms;
  • Camera frames are released after per-frame processing and never written to disk;
  • You grant or revoke permissions yourself in system settings;
  • We follow a data-minimization principle: any data that can stay on your device is never uploaded.
